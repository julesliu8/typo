Korean words for attitudes and feelings

Please describe the situations naturally in Korean. More than one expression may fit a situation. We are interested in overlapping meanings as well as differences. There is no single correct word.

First give your own expressions in Part A. Complete that part for your session before opening Part B. You can leave anything blank or say that a situation is unclear. The English prompts and draft paraphrases are research materials to be checked, not definitions you have to accept.

This file works offline and sends no data anywhere. Answers stay in the open page until you download them. Download before closing; you can load the saved JSON file to continue. A downloaded file contains all parts, including hidden parts.

Part A · Your own expressions

For each situation, first say whether it is clear. Then give one or more complete Korean sentences. A short or longer expression is fine. Do not look for a particular word. If you change a situation to make it understandable, record the change.

Part B · Rate each expression independently

Only open the word lists after completing Part A for this session.

Make a complete Korean sentence with each word if possible. Rate that sentence in the stated situation. Multiple words can all receive high ratings. You may change endings, tense and politeness level. If a word needs a different meaning or a change to the situation, explain.

Naturalness: 1 very unnatural · 2 rather unnatural · 3 possible with conditions · 4 natural · 5 very natural · ? unsure or unfamiliar. Meaning fit is a separate judgment. A polite formula may be usable without stating an actual feeling.

Part C · Check proposed meanings

Work with a sentence you have already given. The proposed English paraphrases below are preliminary research hypotheses. You can reject, shorten, expand or rewrite them. It is fine if two different words overlap.

Whole paraphrase review

Choose an expression, fix a particular sentence and situation, and check the whole paraphrase. Then mark each line as necessary, usual only, optional, incorrect, or unsure. Give a new example or counterexample. WANT items are diagnostic frames, not decompositions of WANT.

Component checks

For each word independently, can the stated component be absent while your sentence is still true? Please give a Korean sentence, not just a dictionary form.

N = necessary in this use · T = usual association but not necessary · O = may be present or absent · W = incorrect or wrong meaning · ? = unsure. These options do not force words into separate categories.

Part D · Sentence forms and related words

The facilitator can complete these after the meaning checks. First give the most natural sentence; then try an alternative without changing the intended meaning. An alternative may be equally good. Do not invent a sentence if none feels natural.

Pilot materials, not a validated measurement scale. The source scenario labels, proposed categories and expected answers are intentionally not presented as solutions.
