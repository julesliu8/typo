In this article, we look at fourth infinitive verb rections. All the verbs on this page will require verbs to be turned into nouns before they can be used in the verb chain. You do this by using the ending -minen , which is the marker of the fourth infinitive . Verbs ending in -minen can be called nounalized verbs . These nounalized verbs will be added to the chain in a certain case. For example, rakastaa will require the partitive case ( Rakastan ratsasta mista = I love horse riding) and pitää will require the mistä-form aka the elative ( Pidän ratsasta misesta = I like horse riding).
