To begin with, I would say URESHIT - unlike TANOSHII --- always requires a
thought. It would be odd to say "Watashi wa kyoo nazeka URESHI (71 feel URESHI
today, I don't know why)", although it would be natural to say "Watashi wa kyoo nazeka
TANOSHII (I feel TANOSHII today, I don't know why)". One feels URESHII "with
something" or "about something", that is, one thinks about something and one feels
URESHII because of this. URESHI has to do with the real occurrence of the event.
The adjective TANOSHII, however, can also be used to describe a certain mood, not
necessarily linked with any thought.
