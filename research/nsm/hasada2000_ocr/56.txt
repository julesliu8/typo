There are, however, instances where "yorokobu" may be used with the first person.
This is the case where the verb is in the progressive aspect (indicated by its gerundive
form followed by the auxiliary 'ir'). This is because one could describe one's emotional
state dispassionately if the emotion is the one which lasts for a certain time or which has
