7.4. NATSUKASHI
In the last section of this chapter, we will examine the meaning of the word
NATSUKASHI. Strictly speaking, NATSUKASHI is different from URESHIT,
TANOSHI, or UCHOOTEN, since it does not necessarily refer to "good events". I
include the word in this chapter since it implies the existence of "good feelings" as a
result.
Unlike TANOSHI, but like URESHIT and UCHOOTEN, NATSUKASHI has only
the subjective use:" (Watashi) wa [Y ga] NATSUKASHI (I) fel NATSUKASHI
[about X]).
