Thus, to summarise, SUKI is the most generally used word, while AL(SURU) and
KOK(SURU) involve more specific sets of attitudes and feling; hence the explication
for SUKI is more brief and general than that for either Al(SURU) or KOK(SURU). As
for the difference between KON(SURU) and Al(SURU), Al(SURU) refers to a more
calm, quiet, clear, and long-term affectionate feeling than KOI(SURU), which refers to
some intense feeling where one seeks after something strongly. Al(SURU) is a calmer
fecling than the feeling of KOK(SURU). However, unlike the feeling of Al(SURU),
KOI(SURU) is not a long-lasting feeling, despite its intensity. Moreover, AI(SURU),
unlike KOK(SURU), is accompanied not only by pleasure but also by concepts like self-
sacrifice.
