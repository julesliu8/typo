I think that SHIAWASE refers to something like a deep appreciation felt towards
something. In example (1), Kuroyanagi appreciates her mother for not br. • her when
she was expelled from an elementary school. In example (3), Umemiya is thackful for
the happy circumstances that allow her to do her favourite modelling job. In example (4),
Oohashi is grateful that the period in which he was a child was when mothers could give
plenty of affection to their children. In example (5), Ishida confesses that she valued the
time when she could eat noodles with her family at the end of the year as SHIAWASE.
In example (6), Yamashita enjoyed the chance to eat the best crab while working in his
job, and described his feeling at that time as SHIA.WASE.
Even though Mizutani and Mizutani (1990: 40) suggest that feelings like SHIAWASE
refer to a state which lasts "for some time", there is a wide variation in the time periods
covered by this phrase. In cases like example (3) or (4) the subject has this SHIAWASE
emotion for a rather long time. In the case of examples (5) or (6) the subjects felt
SHIAWASE suddenly while they were eating food.
