Thus, to summarise, SUKI is the most generally used word, while Al(SURU) and
KOI(SURU) involve more specific sets of attitudes and feelings; hence the explication
for SUKI is more brief and general than that for either Al(SURU) or KOI(SURU). As
for the difference between KOI(SURU) and AI(SURU), AI(SURU) refers to a more
calm, quiet, clear, and long-term affectionate feeling than KOI(SURU), which refers to
some intense feeling where one seeks after something strongly. Al(SURU) is a calmer
feeling than the feeling of KOI(SURU). However, unlike the feeling of A(SURU),
KOI(SURU) is not a long-lasting feeling, despite its intensity. Moreover, AI(SURU),
unlike KOI(SURU), is accompanied not only by pleasure but also by concepts like self-
sacrifice.
