Hanrahan (1989: 120) is also right in pointing out that men are more likely to use
HORERU, rather than KOI(SURU) for expressing affectionate feelings towards women
because it has an "active" connotation. He says that women would rather use
KOI(SURU) than HORERU, since KOI(SURU) has a "passive" connotation and
traditionally women do not make the first approach in a romance.

is a verb which is prototypically used in the first person exclusively by men in
expressing sexual desire, a spontaneous feeling of physical attraction towards another
