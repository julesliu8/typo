6. (II) I FEEL SOMETHING GOOD TOWARDS SOMEBODY
and related concepts
Suki, Koi(suru), Ai(suru), / Horeru, Akogareru
Koishii, Itoshii, Itooshii, Ijirashit, Kenage
In this chapter, we will deal first of all, with Japanese emotion words which are
usually translated into English by verbs such as "like" or "love". Then follows a
discussion on adjectives which refer to affective emotions.
Before we go into a detailed discussiun of each word, it would probably be useful to
sketch the main differences between the three words SUKI (na-adjective), KOI(SURU)
(ko": noun; "oisun": verb), and Al(SURU) ("ai": noun; "asur";: verb). They are
often used, but it is difficult to describe which one corresponds to English 'love' or 'like'
in various contexts.
The word SUKI has a wide semantic range. While the verbs AISURU and
KOISURU cannot be used with concrete inanimate objects or activities, SUKI can.
(I love hima.)
