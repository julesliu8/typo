Carol Genetti

Professor Emerita

https://carolgenetti.academicwebsite.com/

About

I retired from UC Santa Barbara in 2020 and moved to New York University Abu Dhabi in the United Arab Emirates. For more information, please see my website: https://carolgenetti.academicwebsite.com/

Specializations:

Language documentation and description, endangered languages, phonology, morphology, syntax, typology, language change, Tibeto-Burman and Himalayan languages.
