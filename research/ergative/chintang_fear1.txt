Kipma is a lexicalized infinitive of the verb kitt- 'fear', and kat- means 'come up', so lit. 'fearing comes up'. Almost all experiencer predicates of Chintang are based on this pattern (emotional noun + verb, usually kat- 'come up' or katt- 'bring up'). Kipma kat- is more frequent than kitt- and is mostly used without a specific stimulus (so it mostly means 'be afraid' rather than 'be afraid of').
