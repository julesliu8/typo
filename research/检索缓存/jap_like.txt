The Valency Patterns Leipzig online database - Home Languages Verb meanings All coding frames Microroles All alternations ki-ni-iru Language : Japanese (standard) Contributors : Hideki Kishimoto and Taro Kageyama Unknown verb Verb meaning : LIKE [like] Comment : Ki-ni-iru has a complex form in its origin, but is now lexicalized into a single predicate. It can take three different case marking patterns of "nominative-accusative" (cf. ex. 14), "dative-nominative" (cf. ex. 102), and "nominative-nominative" (cf. ex. 103).
