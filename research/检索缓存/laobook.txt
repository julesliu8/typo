A Grammar of Lao – Nick Enfield BOOKS A Grammar of Lao Year: 2007 Publisher: Mouton de Gruyter, Berlin Author: N. J. Enfield Lao is the national language of Laos, and is also spoken widely in Thailand and Cambodia. It is a tone language of the Tai-Kadai family (Southwestern Tai branch). Lao is an extreme example of the isolating, analytic language type. This book is the most comprehensive grammatical description of Lao to date. It describes and analyses the important structures of the language, including classifiers, sentence-final particles, and serial verb constructions.
