  Accessed on .) Basic info Coordinates: 12.9, -7.65 . Genealogy (as given in WALS , with corrections by Valentin Vydrin). Family: Mande, genus: Manding. Macro-area: Sub-Saharan Africa. Bambara (< Manding < Western Mande < Mande < Niger-Congo) is spoken mainly in Mali (West Africa) by more than 16 million people. Grammar notes Bambara is a tonal language with two basic tones, low and high. The basic word order is S AUX (O) V X, where S is a subject, V is a verb, AUX is an auxiliary word expressing grammatical semantics of tense, aspect, mode and polarity (in the Mandeist tradition, AUX are named "predicative markers"), O is a direct object (whose absence makes the verb intransitive), X is an oblique (indirect object or adjunct), most often represented by a postpositional phrase. The word order in NP is N2 – N1 (N1 is the head noun, N2 is the dependent noun), N – Adj (the adjectival modifier follows the head noun). There is no morphological case on nouns. Verb lemmas Lemmas are shown in the form of bare stems. Glossing abbreviations ABSTR — suffix of abstract meaning; ART — tonal article; CNTRL — postposition with the meaning of control; COP — copula; EQU — copula in equative non-verbal sentence; INF — infinitive; INTR — intransitive; IPFV — imperfective; NMLZ — suffix of nominalization; PFV — perfective; PN — proper noun; POSS — possessive marker; PRES — presentative; PRIV — privative suffix; PROG — progressive; PTCP — participle; QUAL — qualitative predicative marker; REFL — reflexive pronoun; RES — resultative; SG — singular; TR — transitive.
